================================================================================
GLOBAL HERO REGISTRATION & MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (GHRMS)
SYSTEM FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION (NON-TECHNICAL GUIDE)
================================================================================

DOCUMENT OVERVIEW:
This document explains the Global Hero Registration & Management System (GHRMS)
in clear, non-technical language. It describes what the system does, who uses it,
the step-by-step processes, screen layouts, and security rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHAT IS GHRMS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Global Hero Registration & Management System (GHRMS) is a centralized web
platform built to license, evaluate, and manage superheroes for public service.

In simple terms, GHRMS works like a modern digital registry for superheroes—
similar to how a Department of Motor Vehicles licenses drivers or a government
medical board certifies doctors, but with specialized tools to:

1. Safeguard Secret Identities:
   Keep civilian legal names, safehouse addresses, and emergency family contacts
   strictly locked away inside a digital vault away from the public eye.

2. Standardize Power & Threat Ratings:
   Classify what each hero can do, how strong they are, and what safety limits
   exist (from localized Street level to world-shaking Cosmic level).

3. Streamline Intake Review & Approvals:
   Allow city review officers to check identity documents, ask applicants to fix
   errors (such as a blurry ID photo), and grant official legal licenses.

4. Equip Field Police & Checkpoints:
   Allow police patrol officers at street checkpoints to scan a hero's digital
   badge on a smartphone or tablet and immediately confirm their active legal
   standing and safety warnings in under 1 second.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM USERS & CLEARANCE TIERS (WHO USES THE SYSTEM?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access is controlled through 5 clearance levels, just like security badges in a
government facility:

CLEARANCE LEVEL 5: SUPREME COMMANDER (Full Citywide Control & Audit)
CLEARANCE LEVEL 4: ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER (Staff & System Management)
CLEARANCE LEVEL 3: INTAKE REGISTRAR (Application Review & Licensing)
CLEARANCE LEVEL 2: TACTICAL ASSESSOR (Power & Threat Tier Rating)
CLEARANCE LEVEL 1: HERO APPLICANT / OPERATIVE (Self-Service Registration)
FIELD ACCESS:      POLICE PATROL & SENTINEL GUARDS (Street Checkpoint Scanner)

ROLE BREAKDOWN:

[A] Hero Applicant / Operative (Clearance Level 1)
- Registers an account using their superhero alias (e.g., "Apex" or "Solaris").
- Completes a 4-step guided intake form.
- Saves progress as a draft at any time and finishes later.
- Uploads government ID cards, academy diplomas, and a face photo.
- Tracks review progress in real-time.
- If an officer requests a fix (e.g. expired document), reads the officer's note,
  updates the record, and clicks Resubmit.
- Once approved, receives a digital hero card with a dynamic, tamper-proof QR code.
- Submits post-battle damage reports after missions.

[B] Intake Registrar & Tactical Assessor (Clearance Levels 2 & 3)
- Works from an intake queue of pending applications.
- Opens applications in a split-screen view to verify identity papers.
- Unlocks the confidential civilian identity vault when matching real names with
  uploaded government IDs (every unlock event is permanently logged).
- Marks documents as "Verified" or "Rejected".
- Calibrates power output ratings (1–100) and assigned threat tiers.
- Sends applications back to the hero with specific instructions if items are missing.
- Grants official licenses (format: GHRMS-LIC-YYYY-XXXX) or rejects invalid files.

[C] System Administrator & Supreme Commander (Clearance Levels 4 & 5)
- Monitors citywide hero readiness and a live threat radar map.
- Creates, updates, and disables registrar staff accounts.
- Reviews the permanent, tamper-evident audit ledger (which tracks every view of a
  secret identity).
- Issues emergency citywide alerts or containment orders if an operative goes rogue.

[D] Street Police & Sentinel Checkpoint Guards (Field Access)
- Uses a mobile tablet or scanner at street perimeters.
- Scans a hero's badge QR code or enters their 4-character ID.
- Receives an instant Green (Approved) or Red (Unlicensed/Suspended) verdict.
- Views authorized powers, operating sectors, and emergency medical weaknesses.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE 5 MAIN SYSTEM PORTALS (SCREENS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Hero Registration Wizard (/register)
   - Step 1: Civilian Details (Legal name, birthday, emergency contact, face photo).
   - Step 2: Hero Identity (Callsign, power category, weaknesses, combat style).
   - Step 3: Power Ratings (Output 1–100, combat skill 1–100, control level).
   - Step 4: Supporting Documents (Government ID and qualification diplomas).
   - Includes a "Save Draft" button so applicants never lose their work.

2. Hero Operative Dashboard (/hero)
   - Real-time progress bar showing if the file is in Draft, Submitted, or Approved.
   - If changes are needed, displays an amber alert banner with the officer's note.
   - Displays an accredited digital field badge with a dynamic QR code that refreshes
     every 30 seconds to prevent counterfeit screenshots.
   - Post-battle incident drawer to report mission damage or injuries.

3. Registrar Review Desk (/registrar)
   - Filterable list of all applicants in the city.
   - Split-screen view to preview documents, photos, and power ratings side-by-side.
   - One-click buttons to:
     * "Request Corrections" (sends file back with a note)
     * "Verify Identity" (marks civilian credentials authenticated)
     * "Approve License" (generates official municipal license number)
     * "Reject Registration" (denies non-compliant applications)

4. Command Center (/admin)
   - Citywide threat radar, staff user administration, and the permanent activity log.

5. Sentinel Street Checkpoint (/sentinel)
   - Rapid camera scanner for police to scan hero badges and view safety protocols.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PATH A: STANDARD SUCCESSFUL REGISTRATION
1. Application: Hero visits /register, fills out all 4 steps, and uploads documents.
2. Submission: Hero clicks "Submit Registration". Status becomes "Submitted", the file
   enters the city review queue, and civilian details are locked in the vault.
3. Review: A registrar officer opens the file ("Under Review") and checks the passport.
4. Vetting: The officer views the decrypted civilian name, confirms it matches the ID,
   and marks the document "Verified".
5. Licensing: The officer clicks "Approve License". A license number is generated
   (e.g., GHRMS-LIC-2026-9281), and the hero's badge illuminates active green.

PATH B: HANDLING MISTAKES (THE CORRECTION LOOP)
1. Issue Found: Reviewing officer notices an uploaded ID is expired or blurry.
2. Officer Requests Fix: Officer clicks "Request Corrections" and enters instructions
   (e.g., "The ID uploaded is expired. Please provide a valid passport scan.").
3. Status Updated: Application status changes to "Returned for Correction".
4. Hero Notified: When the hero logs into /hero, an amber alert banner displays the note.
5. Hero Fixes File: Hero replaces the file and clicks "Resubmit".
6. Re-entry: Application returns to the queue as "Submitted" without losing any data.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. APPLICATION STATUS CODES & MEANINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Draft] (Color: Gray)
Meaning: Work in progress. Saved by the applicant, not yet submitted to the city.
Action by: Hero Applicant

[Submitted] (Color: Yellow)
Meaning: Sent to the city; waiting in line for an intake officer to review.
Action by: Registrar Officer

[Under Review] (Color: Blue)
Meaning: An officer has opened the file and is currently vetting documents.
Action by: Registrar Officer

[Returned for Correction] (Color: Orange / Amber)
Meaning: Officer found an error or missing document and sent it back with notes.
Action by: Hero Applicant (Fix and Resubmit)

[Verified] (Color: Teal)
Meaning: Identity documents and legal credentials have been verified authentic.
Action by: Registrar Officer

[Approved] (Color: Green)
Meaning: Officially certified and licensed! Cleared for field duty.
Action by: Hero & Field Police Checkpoints

[Rejected] (Color: Red)
Meaning: Application denied due to fraud, safety hazard, or Accord violations.
Action by: None (File closed)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SECURITY & PRIVACY EXPLAINED SIMPLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The "Digital Safety Deposit Box" (AES-256 Vault):
   Civilian identities (real names, home safehouses, family contacts) are encrypted
   with military-grade digital locks into an isolated vault. Public superhero personas
   are visible to staff, but real civilian names can only be unlocked by authorized
   officers with Level 3+ clearance, and every view is permanently recorded.

2. The "Tamper-Evident Flight Recorder" (Audit Ledger):
   Every administrative action (viewing secret identities, approving licenses, editing
   records) is recorded in a cryptographically chained log. Just like an airplane's
   black box, nobody—not even IT administrators—can secretly alter or erase records.

3. The "Anti-Screenshot" Digital Badge (Dynamic QR Code):
   The QR code on a hero's phone regenerates every 30 seconds (like a banking 2FA app).
   A screenshot taken minutes earlier will be rejected immediately by police scanners.

7. THREAT TIER SCALE (HOW POWERS ARE CLASSIFIED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Tier 0: Cosmic]   -- Planetary-scale, reality altering or stellar manipulation.
[Tier 1: Extreme]  -- Continental or multi-city impact; extreme devastation risk.
[Tier 2: High]     -- City-district scale; heavy artillery and major structural impact.
[Tier 3: Moderate] -- Neighborhood or multi-block scope (Standard field operative).
[Tier 4: Low]      -- Specialized or single-building tactical scope.
[Tier 5: Street]   -- Hand-to-hand, minor enhancement, localized neighborhood defense.

METRICS CAPTURED:
- Power Output Level: Scale of 1 to 100 measuring physical or energetic yield.
- Combat Rating: Scale of 1 to 100 measuring martial training and tactical acumen.
- Power Control Level: Novice, Competent, Mastered, or Absolute.
- Operative Standing: Hero (Primary operative) or Sidekick (Apprentice in training).
